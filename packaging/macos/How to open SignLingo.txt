How to open SignLingo
=====================

1. Drag SignLingo into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open Applications and double-click SignLingo.

   The first time, your Mac says it can't check SignLingo for malware.
   That's because SignLingo is a free student project, not one registered
   with Apple. It runs only on your computer and never uploads video.

   macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer:
     - Click "Done" on the warning.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to "SignLingo was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway".
     - Enter your password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

   macOS 14 (Sonoma):
     - Right-click (or Control-click) SignLingo and choose "Open".
     - Click "Open" in the box that appears.

   You only do this once. After that SignLingo opens normally.

3. When SignLingo asks to use the camera, click "Allow".
   Changed your mind later? System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.

Needs macOS 14 or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
